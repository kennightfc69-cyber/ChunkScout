Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-19

ChunkScout is built so that you can use every tool without an account and without giving us personal information.

What stays on your device

Some preferences are saved in your browser’s local storage so they’re there next time: your last seed and edition on the seed map, map pins, favorite tools and recently used tools. This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

What our hosting and analytics see

ChunkScout is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical request data (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to deliver and protect the site. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It doesn’t use cookies and doesn’t track you across sites. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Fonts

Pages load the Space Grotesk and JetBrains Mono fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google’s servers. See Google’s privacy policy.

Shareable links

Some tools have a Copy link button. The link contains only that tool’s settings (for example a circle size or a beacon level), never personal information.

Cookies

ChunkScout currently sets no advertising or analytics cookies. If we add Google Analytics or advertising (such as Google AdSense) in the future, we’ll update this page first, and where the law requires it we’ll ask for your consent before any such cookies are set. Third-party vendors, including Google, may then use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You’d be able to opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.

Emails

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and we don’t add you to any mailing list.

Children

ChunkScout doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

We’ll update the date at the top when this policy changes. Questions: contact@chunkscout.com.
