Hollow, solid and wall thickness

Hollow keeps a shell of the thickness you choose. Shells are measured across block faces, so they’re watertight. Cylinders, cones and domes can be open at the top or bottom: an open bottom leaves out the floor so the shape can sit on the ground. For arches, the thickness is how deep the curved band of the arch is.

Building from the layer blueprints

  1. Mark the north-west corner of the footprint in-game. Row 1 is the north edge and column x1 is the west edge of every layer.
  2. Build layer 1 (the bottom) from its blueprint, then move up one layer at a time.
  3. All layers (.txt) gives you written instructions with every run of blocks, which is handy on a phone. All layers (ZIP) gives you one image per layer.

How the shapes are calculated

A block is part of a shape when the centre of the block is inside the ideal shape. For example, a cone keeps blocks where the horizontal distance from the axis is no more than the base radius × (1 − height fraction). All sizes are up to 160 blocks per side, and the work runs in a background thread so the page stays responsive.
