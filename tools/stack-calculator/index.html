Stack sizes in Minecraft

Most blocks and items stack to 64. A smaller group stacks to 16, and anything with durability, contents or special data doesn’t stack at all.

Stack sizeExamples
64Most blocks, ingots, food, arrows, redstone dust, seeds
16Ender pearls, snowballs, eggs, signs, empty buckets, banners, honey bottles, armor stands
1Tools, weapons, armor, potions, beds, boats, minecarts, totems, filled buckets, enchanted books, shulker boxes

How the calculator works

full stacks = floor(items ÷ stack size)
left over = items mod stack size
slots = ceil(items ÷ stack size)
shulkers = ceil(slots ÷ 27)
double chests = ceil(slots ÷ 54)

A partly-filled stack still takes up a whole slot, which is why slots are rounded up.

FAQ

How many items are in a shulker box?

27 stacks. That’s 1,728 items for 64-stackables or 432 for 16-stackables.

Can I put a shulker box inside a shulker box?

No. Shulker boxes can’t hold other shulker boxes, but chests, barrels and your ender chest can.

Is this the same on Bedrock?

Yes. Stack sizes and container sizes match between Java and Bedrock for the items listed here.
