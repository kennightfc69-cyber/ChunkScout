Beacon levels, blocks and range

A beacon sits on top of a stepped pyramid. Each level adds a square layer two blocks wider than the one above it.

LevelLayersBlocksRangeEffect duration
13×3920 blocks11 s

49×9 + 7×7 + 5×5 + 3×316450 blocks17 s
blocks in layer n (from the top) = (2n + 1)² · range = 10 × level + 10 · duration = 9 s + 2 s × level

The range is a square around the beacon, not a circle, and it reaches up to the top of the world. Effects are re-applied every 4 seconds, so they never run out while you stay in range. One block of iron, gold, emerald or diamond is 9 ingots or gems. A netherite block is 9 netherite ingots, and each netherite ingot takes 4 netherite scrap and 4 gold ingots. Bedrock Edition uses the same pyramid sizes, but its range can reach about one block further on some sides.

Several beacons on one base

Beacons placed side by side can share one pyramid. Each layer only needs to be two blocks wider than the layer above on every side, so for a block of beacons that is R rows by C columns, layer n needs (R + 2n) × (C + 2n) blocks. A 2 × 2 group of level-4 beacons needs 216 blocks instead of 656 for four separate pyramids, and lets you run several different primary effects at once.

How to build and activate a beacon

  1. Craft the beacon: 5 glass, 3 obsidian and a Nether Star (dropped by the Wither).
  2. Build the bottom layer first, centred where you want the beacon, then each smaller layer on top.
  3. Place the beacon in the centre of the top layer. It needs a clear view of the sky (glass and other transparent blocks are fine).
  4. Open the beacon, put in one iron ingot, gold ingot, emerald, diamond or netherite ingot, and choose your powers.

FAQ

Can I mix block types?

Yes. Any mix of iron, gold, emerald, diamond and netherite blocks works, even within one layer.

What is the cheapest full beacon?

164 iron blocks, which is 1,476 iron ingots. Iron farms make this easy.

Why isn’t my beacon beam showing?

Something opaque is above the beacon, or the pyramid isn’t complete and centred. Every block in each layer must be a mineral block.
