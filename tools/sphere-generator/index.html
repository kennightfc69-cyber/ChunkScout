How to build a sphere in Minecraft

  1. Choose the diameter and whether you want it hollow or solid. Use the 3D preview to check the shape.
  2. Find the centre of your build site and mark it. Odd diameters have a single centre block; even diameters have a 2×2 centre.
  3. Start at layer 1 (the bottom) and work up. Each layer is a circle: use the layer blueprint and its “block positions” list, which gives the runs of blocks in every row.
  4. Turn on Highlight this layer in 3D to see where the layer sits in the whole sphere. For big builds, download all layers as a ZIP and keep the images open on another screen.

How the sphere is calculated

Each block is a 1×1×1 cube. A block belongs to the solid sphere when the centre of the cube is inside the ideal sphere:

((x + 0.5 − d/2)² + (y + 0.5 − d/2)² + (z + 0.5 − d/2)²) ≤ (d/2)²

For a hollow sphere, ChunkScout keeps the solid blocks that are within your chosen thickness of the outside, counting steps across block faces. That makes the shell watertight. The generator runs in a background thread, so even 200-block spheres don’t freeze the page.

Tips for big spheres

  • Build the widest layer (the middle) first as a guide ring, then work outward in both directions.
  • Scaffolding makes the overhanging lower half much easier to place.
  • Glass spheres underwater: build the shell first, then remove the water inside with sponges or sand.
