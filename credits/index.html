Textures

The block and item pictures on ChunkScout are not Mojang’s textures. They come from Pixel Perfection Community Edition, a fan-made texture pack originally created by Hugh “XSSheep” Rutland and updated by StonePendant, freejusticehere, Stingraych, Nova_Wostra, lazerl0rd and other contributors.

Pixel Perfection is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0).

Changes we made

  • Kept only the first frame of animated textures (water, nether portal, command block).
  • Tinted greyscale grass, leaf and water textures.
  • Combined parts of textures into small icons (a chest front, a framed nether portal, a four-colour wool tile, a beacon) and scaled the grass block for our favicon.
  • Darkened and tiled some textures as page backgrounds.

Our adapted textures are shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence. You can download them from our GitHub repository; the licence text is in assets/tex/LICENSE.txt.

Your own textures

The block gradient generator can also load textures from your own copy of the game or any resource pack. Those files are read only in your browser and are never uploaded or shared by ChunkScout.
